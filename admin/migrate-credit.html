One-Time: Migrate Store Credit to Ledger

Sums each customer's existing unappliedCredit (closed-unresolved orders) and paymentOverage.excessAmount (approved overpayments) and writes the total to users/<uid>.creditBalance, the new authoritative store-credit ledger. Safe to run more than once - it recomputes the full sum each time rather than incrementing, so re-running just corrects the balance back to what the order history says it should be. Delete this page once you've confirmed the balances look right.
