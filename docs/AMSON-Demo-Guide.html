AMSON Demo Guide

Live site: https://amsonpharma.web.app · about 25 minutes · one customer story from order to report

1. Before the client arrives 20 min

  1. Two browsers, not two tabs. Chrome = staff (Branch Manager). Edge or a Chrome Incognito window = customer. Two tabs of one browser share one login.
  2. Close every old tab of the site, then open it fresh in both browsers (Ctrl+F5).
  3. Load stock (important). Only 7 of the 50 products have stock right now, so most show "Out of stock". As the Branch Manager: Inventory → Receive Stock → Upload Batch File → choose AMSON-Demo-Stock-43-Products.xlsx → Confirm Import. A "Stock received" pop-up lists 43 lines.
    • One product (Paracetamol 250mg Susp.) gets only 5 units, so it shows under Low Stock. One (Ibuprofen 400mg) expires in March 2027, so it shows under Near-Expiry and isn't sold retail. Both are on purpose, to demo the alerts.
  4. Customer account: log in the customer browser with a customer whose email is already verified (e.g. biancabeniceabalos@gmail.com). Don't register live: the code email can be slow.
  5. Files on the desktop: any payment screenshot image and any prescription photo (the upload needs an image).
  6. A Lalamove link to paste: https://share.lalamove.com/?demo123 (it must start with share.lalamove.com/).
  7. Practise once: run sections 2-4 quickly before the client comes, so nothing is a first try.
Things the client hasn't sent yet (say "this is where your … goes"):
  • The QR Ph code on the payment page shows a placeholder.
  • Store hours, address, hotline: the footer leaves them out, and the chat says hours "aren't posted online yet".
  • The supplier is named "Supplier Sample". Rename it in Suppliers first if you want it to look real; if you do, change the supplier column in the stock file to match.

2. The customer orders online 6 min

Customer browser.

  1. Home page: point out the 8 "Shop by Need" sections in the bar under the search box.
  2. Products: pick a section, set a price range, tick "In stock only", sort by price, then search a name. Show a product page and its stock.
  3. Prescription items: Antibiotics (Rx) items carry a red Rx tag everywhere: list, cart, checkout, orders.
  4. Chat (bottom right): ask "how to pay?" and "delivery fee?". Then tap Talk to staff, which hands the chat to a person.
  5. Normal order: add 2 non-Rx products → cart → change a quantity → Proceed to Checkout → address + delivery schedule → payment page → upload the screenshot + type a reference no. → Submit.
  6. My Orders shows "Order Pending Verification", and the bell shows the confirmation. An email also arrives.
"Customers order and pay any time. Nothing is confirmed until a pharmacist checks the payment."

3. Staff verify, invoice and deliver 6 min

Staff browser (Branch Manager).

  1. Dashboard: the new order already shows; nobody refreshed. Click Day / Week / Month and the bell.
  2. Online Orders → Payment Verification: open the order, look at the screenshot, click Approve Payment. It gets an SI No. and stock goes down by itself.
  3. Online Orders tab → eye icon: the full order (contact, address, items, history).
  4. Book Delivery: paste the Lalamove link + the fee → Dispatched. In the customer browser, open the order: it shows Track with Lalamove.
"Every approved order gets a BIR-style SI number automatically, in sequence."

Optional: a payment problem (2 min)

Place a second order. In Payment Verification choose Underpayment, enter the amount received → On Hold. The customer sees "On Hold - Balance Due" and re-uploads. Staff then approve it.

Prescription order (3 min)

  1. Customer: order an Rx item. Checkout asks for the prescription photo, and the order is pick-up only.
  2. Staff: approve it in Payment Verification → "Ready for Pick-up". In the Online Orders tab click Mark as Picked Up. The customer is notified.

4. Walk-in sale at the counter (POS) 3 min

  1. Open Inventory in the staff browser first (keep it visible), then open POS. A second computer works too.
  2. Cash sale: add 2 products → Cash → amount → Complete Sale → receipt with SI No. → print.
  3. Live inventory: switch back to Inventory. The batch quantity has already changed.
  4. Payment and Rx checks: try G-Cash without a reference number, which is blocked. Try an Rx item without ticking "RX Verified", also blocked.
"POS and online share one stock count, so you never sell what you don't have."

5. Stock, suppliers, wholesale 5 min

  • Inventory: point out the tiles and the Low Stock / Out of Stock / Near-Expiry / Expired tabs. Stock goes out earliest expiry first (FEFO).
  • Receive Stock: mention you loaded 43 products with one Excel file earlier, and show the ? template button.
  • Purchase Orders: Create Purchase Order → Upload Batch File, or add items → Create. Then receive it with one item short → status Discrepancy + a bell alert.
  • Write-off a few units with a reason (expired / damaged).
  • Wholesale Accounts: a buyer with LTO and business permit → Process Wholesale Order → download the invoice (it shows batch and expiry).

6. Control and reports 4 min

  • Products: change a price with a reason. Only the Branch Manager can change prices.
  • Audit Log: that price change shows old → new price + reason, as do stock received, voids, status changes and who did them.
  • Reports: Daily Sales for today → PDF and Excel. The totals include the sales you just made.
  • Staff Management: the 3 roles. Sales Staff only see POS; if they type the dashboard address they are turned away.
  • Settings → Store Announcement: save a banner and show it on the shop.
"Everything is in one system instead of the notebook and spreadsheets: online orders, POS, stock, wholesale, reports."

7. If something goes wrong

You seeDo this
Products empty / keeps loadingWait 10 seconds, then refresh. Still empty? Close all tabs of the site and reopen one.
Page looks old (no Rx tags, old buttons)Ctrl+F5 once.
Logged in as the wrong personYou used two tabs of the same browser. Staff in one browser, customer in another.
"Out of stock" on a productThat product has no stock (or only near-expiry stock). Pick another, or Receive Stock for it.
Login says "Invalid domain for site key"The reCAPTCHA checkbox key (6LfcDXIt…) needs amsonpharma.web.app in its domain list at google.com/recaptcha/admin. Fix this before the demo.
Code / notification email doesn't arriveCheck spam. The free email service sends up to 200 a month, so don't register new accounts during the demo.
Approve shows a "Check before approving" warningThe price on the order no longer matches the catalog (you changed it after ordering). Explain it's a safety check, or order again.
Staff page shows only a spinnerNormal for about a second while it checks the role. If it stays, refresh.
Don't press Settings → Clear Test Data during the demo. It deletes orders and stock.

8. After the demo, before go-live tomorrow

  1. Get the QR Ph image, store hours, address, hotline and FDA LTO no. from the client.
  2. Settings → Clear Test Data (only works before Nov 15), then load the real products and stock.
  3. Rename or replace "Supplier Sample" with the real suppliers.
