Sales Staff: POS Cheat Sheet

Log in at amsonpharma.web.app. You land on the POS. You can use the POS and view products only.

Make a sale

  1. Search the product → set the quantity → Add. Repeat for each item.
  2. Check Current Sale (items, quantities, total).
  3. Choose the payment method:
    • Cash: type the cash received; the change shows.
    • G-Cash / QR Ph, bank, card: type the reference no. (required).
  4. Prescription item? Check the doctor's prescription, then tick RX Verified (required).
  5. Complete Sale → check the receipt → Print.

Reprint a receipt

Today's Sales (bottom of the POS) → open the sale → Print.

You can't void a sale. If you made a mistake, call the Technical Staff or Branch Manager. They void it with a reason, and the stock goes back.

Good to know

  • Only products in stock appear in the POS. Missing one? Tell the Technical Staff.
  • Near-expiry stock isn't sold retail. The system picks the batch that expires first for you.
  • Not your computer? Untick Remember me when logging in, and log out when you leave.
  • No internet: phone hotspot, or the paper booklet; enter the sale when it's back.

Technical Staff: Cheat Sheet

You handle online orders, stock, wholesale and voids. You can't change prices or staff accounts.

Online orders (Orders)

  1. Payment Verification: open the order → check the screenshot and reference no. → Approve Payment (gets an SI No.; stock goes down).
  2. Payment wrong? Choose Underpayment / Invalid Payment → order goes On Hold; the customer fixes it within 7 days.
  3. Rx order: check the prescription → approve (or Reject Prescription with a reason) → Ready for Pick-up → Mark as Picked Up when collected.
  4. Delivery: Online Orders tab → Book Delivery → paste the share.lalamove.com link + the fee → Dispatched.
  5. The eye icon shows the full order.

Stock (Inventory)

  • Receive Stock: supplier, receipt/DR no., date → product, batch no., expiry, quantity. Many items? Upload Batch File (the ? button gives the template).
  • Write Off Stock: expired/damaged, with a reason.
  • Purchase Orders: create → when it arrives, receive what actually came (short = Discrepancy).
  • Stock Count: Start Count → enter counts → Submit Count. The Branch Manager finalizes it.
  • Watch the tiles: Low Stock, Out of Stock, Near-Expiry, Expired.

Wholesale (Wholesale Accounts)

  • Process Order: items (prices fill in), payment method; tick Free (₱0) on an item if given free, with a reason → download the Invoice.
  • Goods picked up/delivered → clock icon → Mark Released.
  • Exchange → clock icon → Exchange (no refunds; equal or higher value).

Void a POS sale

POS → Today's Sales (or Orders → Walk-in Orders) → open the sale → Void → items + reason → Branch Manager types their email and password.

Branch Manager: Cheat Sheet

Everything Technical Staff can do (see their sheet), plus the items below.

Prices (Products)

  • Only you can change prices. Edit the product → change the price → give a reason (shown in the Audit Log).
  • Lock prices on a product to stop accidental changes. Unlock prices first to edit (🔒 shows in the list).
  • New products: one at a time, or Upload Batch File (Excel).

Staff (Staff Management)

  • Add Staff Account: name, email, role (Technical Staff / Sales Staff).
  • Someone leaves → Deactivate. They're signed out everywhere at once.

Approvals

  • Voids by Technical Staff: type your email and password in their void window.
  • Stock Count: review the differences → Finalize with a reason. Only then does stock change.

Checking the business

  • Dashboard: Day / Week / Month / Year; export.
  • Reports: Daily Sales and others → PDF or Excel.
  • Audit Log: who did what (prices, voids, write-offs, stock, orders); search, dates, export.
  • Settings → Store Announcement: a banner on the shop.
Clear Test Data (Settings) deletes the practice orders, sales and stock. Use it once, on go-live day, after training. It only works until Nov 15.
Free-plan limits: about 200 emails a month (sign-up codes and order notices). If emails stop, orders still work.
