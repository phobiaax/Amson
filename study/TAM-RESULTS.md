## How the numbers were computed

- **Item weighted mean** = Σ(f · w) ÷ 3. Example for Q5: (2 × 5 + 1 × 4) ÷ 3 = 14 ÷ 3 = **4.67**.
- **Construct mean** = the average of its item means. Ease of Use: (4.67 + 5.00 + 4.67 + 5.00) ÷ 4 = **4.83**.
- **Overall** = the average of the 3 construct means: (5.00 + 4.83 + 5.00) ÷ 3 = **4.94**.
- **Rank:** tied means share a rank, and the next mean takes the next number (1, 1, 2), as the Statistical Tool section describes ("placed in sequence below it"). If your adviser uses the other style, Ease of Use becomes Rank 3.
- **Interpretation:** 4.21–5.00 = Strongly Agree (Chart 1.1).
